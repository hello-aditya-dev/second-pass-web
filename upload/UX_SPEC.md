# UX Specification

## Goal
A reader should understand something useful before they feel monetized.

The site should be remembered for:
- speed
- headline hierarchy
- evidence close to claims
- excellent mobile tables
- predictable interaction
- restrained commercial presence

## Homepage
Above fold:
1. masthead
2. lead story
3. `/ NOW`

No ad.

Then:
- `/ SECOND PASS`
- natural commercial break
- topic coverage
- `/ DATA`
- optional later commercial break
- research
- `/ BRIEF`

## Article anatomy
1. section / format
2. headline
3. dek
4. author / published / updated
5. FIRST PASS
6. `/ SECOND PASS`
7. evidence/figures/tables
8. sources
9. change log
10. `/ END`
11. follow-up/related
12. `/ BRIEF`

## Reading
Desktop prose measure: 680–740px.
Long-form body: 19–21px.
Mobile: 18–19px.
Line-height: ~1.6–1.7.

## Desktop rail
Use supporting rails for evidence, navigation, related data, or reading progress.
Do not waste prime reading rail on skyscraper ads.

## Tables
Desktop full-width where useful.
Tablet/mobile horizontally scroll.
Never shrink type until unreadable.

## End state
Clear `/ END`. No infinite-scroll article chain.
