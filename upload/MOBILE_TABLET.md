# Mobile & Tablet

## Grid
Desktop: 12-column concept.
Tablet: 8-column.
Mobile: 4-column.

## Mobile
- compact masthead
- horizontal topic strip
- no sticky commercial unit
- headline/dek/metadata first
- FIRST PASS before any ad
- evidence inline
- charts may break edge-to-edge
- tables scroll
- URLs wrap
- touch targets ~44px+

Ads:
- typical article: one inline
- very long deep dive: max two
- short signal: zero inline
- never sticky/popup/full-screen/video takeover

## Tablet
Center reading column around 650–720px.
Collapse desktop rails inline.
No side-rail ads.
Commercial units appear only at structural section breaks.

## Typography
Use `clamp()` and length-aware layouts. Long headlines must not degrade into one-word lines.

## QA widths
375, 390, 430, 768, 820, 1024, 1440.
