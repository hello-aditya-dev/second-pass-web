# Design System

## Working aesthetic

**Editorial industrialism.**

Black/ink typography, warm paper background, fine rules, one high-energy signal accent.

The site should feel engineered rather than decorated.

## Tokens

Defined in `src/app/globals.css`.

- paper
- ink
- muted
- rule
- signal
- panel

## Typography

Use high-performing system stacks until a deliberate licensed/webfont decision is made.

Editorial hierarchy matters more than brand-font novelty.

## Layout

- max page width around 1280px
- article reading measure around 720px
- dense headline grid on desktop
- single-column reading on mobile

## Motion

Motion should communicate state, never exist as spectacle.

Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Ad readiness

Reserve future ad positions deliberately to minimize layout shift. Do not add empty ad boxes before
an ad product exists.
