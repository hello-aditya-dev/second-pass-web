# Monetization Foundation

Revenue should follow reader trust, not distort it.

## Early order

1. newsletter growth
2. direct sponsor experiments
3. limited display advertising
4. premium ad network when eligible
5. sponsored research with strict labeling
6. later premium data/research products

## UI rules

- no ad directly between headline and article opening
- no sticky unit covering content
- no deceptive native-ad styling
- reserve ad height to prevent layout shift
- label sponsorship
- keep mobile readable

No ad vendor is integrated in this foundation.
