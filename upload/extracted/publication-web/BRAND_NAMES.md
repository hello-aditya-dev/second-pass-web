# Brand Name Shortlist

These are creative candidates, not legal clearance. Before purchase/launch, perform:
- trademark search in target jurisdictions;
- domain registrar check;
- social handle check;
- search-engine collision review.

## 1. HexFallow — recommended working name

**Feel:** strange, technical, editorial, slightly industrial.  
**Why:** memorable without saying "AI", "tech", "future", "neural", or "daily".  
**Tagline direction:** `Deep systems. Hard evidence.`

## 2. KernelRift

**Feel:** low-level systems + disruption.  
**Tagline:** `Where the stack actually changes.`

## 3. PacketKiln

**Feel:** networking + industrial process.  
**Tagline:** `Technical signals, fired down to facts.`

## 4. RackLore

**Feel:** data-center knowledge passed through hard technical evidence.  
**Tagline:** `The machinery behind modern software.`

## 5. RivetByte

**Feel:** physical infrastructure fused with software.  
**Tagline:** `Compute, systems, and the bolts between them.`

## 6. WireFallow

**Feel:** odd, sparse, infrastructural.  
**Tagline:** `What changed beneath the interface.`

## 7. CacheTithe

**Feel:** weird, memorable, technical-economic.  
**Tagline:** `The cost of every abstraction.`

## 8. FaultMorrow

**Feel:** engineering failure + future systems.  
**Tagline:** `Tomorrow's systems, inspected early.`

## 9. OxideLedger

**Feel:** semiconductors + evidence/accounting.  
**Tagline:** `Compute measured, not marketed.`

## 10. BusbarGhost

**Feel:** electrical/data-center infrastructure, deliberately strange.  
**Tagline:** `The invisible infrastructure of AI.`

## Naming rule

Avoid names built from the exhausted pattern:

`AI + Daily / Insider / Wire / News / Hub / Times / Pulse / Chronicle`

The final brand should be ownable enough that readers can search the name and mean us.
