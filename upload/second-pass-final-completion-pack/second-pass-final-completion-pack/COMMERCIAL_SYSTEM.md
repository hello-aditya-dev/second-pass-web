# Commercial System

Create public routes:
- `/partner`
- `/intelligence`

Keep them discoverable from the footer and relevant CTAs but subordinate to editorial navigation.

## `/partner`
Positioning: `Reach engineers and technical decision-makers without making the publication worse.`

Show honest product categories:
- Founding Partner
- `/BRIEF` Partner
- `/DATA` Partner
- selected premium display
- custom package

No fake traffic, logos, clients or mature-media rate claims.

## `/intelligence`
Positioning: `Technical intelligence for decisions that cannot wait for the market to become obvious.`

Examples:
- AI infrastructure market mapping
- inference economics
- GPU/cloud pricing analysis
- technical competitor analysis
- architecture/supply-chain research
- source-backed custom data work

No file uploads initially.

## Commercial ethics
Sponsor money never buys coverage, conclusions, source suppression, benchmark manipulation or editorial review rights.

## Slot model
Implement public-safe static config for placements:
`home-mid`, `home-late`, `article-mid-1`, `article-mid-2`, `article-end`, `data-sponsor`, `brief-sponsor`.

Kinds: house, direct, programmatic, sponsor.
Labels: ADVERTISEMENT, SUPPORTED BY, HOUSE.

Keep current strict density rules. No first-view, FIRST PASS, evidence/source, sticky mobile, popup or figure/table interruption.
