7. CENTRAL THESIS — FROZEN
────────────────────────────────────────

Preserve:

Peak arithmetic throughput is only one hardware ceiling.

For a workload with arithmetic intensity:

I

and hardware with:

peak compute P

HBM bandwidth B

the simplified Roofline bound is:

P_attainable
<=
min(P_peak, B_mem × I).

The machine-balance point is:

I*
=
P_peak / B_mem.

When:

I < I*

the local HBM-bandwidth roof is tighter under the simplified Roofline model.

When:

I > I*

the compute roof can become tighter.

Do not turn this into:

GPU benchmark prediction.

Do not turn this into:

measured serving performance.
