## AI-cloud / colo / facility engineering

**Subject:** GB300 rack capacity model: powerable racks before procurement

I published a source-backed model for a practical infrastructure question: given a site's actual electrical and thermal capacity, how many GB300-class racks can it operate?

The current NVIDIA GB300 Enterprise RA says the full rack requires up to 142 kW with 72 GPUs.

The release translates that into:
- racks and GPUs per usable IT MW;
- facility-input vs usable-IT capacity;
- PUE/headroom sensitivity;
- thermal-load equivalents;
- stranded racks/GPUs;
- incremental facility requirement.

It includes the CSVs and an editable formula-driven workbook.
