A GPU purchase has a building requirement.

NVIDIA says the GB300 NVL72 full rack can require **up to 142 kW**.

At that reference:
1 MW of usable IT power fits 7 whole racks = 504 GPUs.

But 1 MW of facility input is not the same thing.

In one explicit 4 MW / PUE 1.20 / 90% usable-capacity scenario, the model supports 21 racks.

If 24 are already procured, 216 GPUs are stranded until facility capacity changes.
