# SECOND / PASS / BRIEF

## Summary
NVIDIA's current GB300 NVL72 Enterprise Reference Architecture says a full rack requires up to 142 kW for 72 GPUs. At that upper planning reference, 1 MW of usable IT power supports seven whole racks, or 504 GPUs. A facility-input megawatt is different: in a 4 MW / PUE 1.20 / 90% usable-capacity scenario, the model supports 21 racks and 1,512 GPUs. A 24-rack target needs about 4.544 MW of modeled facility input, otherwise 216 GPUs are stranded. The result makes facility readiness a procurement input, not an afterthought.
