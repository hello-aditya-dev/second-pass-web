# COMMERCIAL HANDOFF

## Product
**AI FACILITY CAPACITY SPRINT**

A buyer-specific research product derived from the flagship, not public sales copy for the article body.

## Inputs
- target accelerator/rack architecture
- target and procured rack counts
- facility capacity definitions
- electrical single-line / critical IT capacity
- rack distribution / busway / PDU limits
- redundancy design
- cooling / heat-rejection capacity
- PUE measurements or planning assumptions
- deployment schedule
- electricity price/tariff structure
- rack acquisition cost if stranded-capital analysis is required.

## Outputs
- normalized usable rack capacity
- electrical bottleneck map
- thermal bottleneck map
- rack-distribution density check
- stranded rack/GPU risk
- incremental facility requirement
- PUE/headroom/redundancy sensitivity
- energy/cost scenarios
- executive decision brief
- source and assumption ledger.

## Boundary
Not electrical-engineering certification, permit design, utility interconnection design or mechanical commissioning. Those require qualified site professionals and project-specific engineering.
