## / SECOND PASS

Research coverage should inspect methodology when methodology determines the result.

A strong-looking score can depend on baseline selection, evaluator design, contamination,
prompting, fine-tuning, or a narrow task definition. The publication should not turn an abstract
into a more confident claim than the authors themselves can defend.

This page does not summarize a specific paper.
