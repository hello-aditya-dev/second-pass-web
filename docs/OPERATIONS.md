# Operations

## Daily
Morning: discover/rank signals and approve research targets.
During day: fast lane for breaking stories; deep lane for data/research.
End: brief queue, product issues, weak-idea kills.

## Product changes
One coherent change → verify → ship → observe.
Avoid month-long redesign branches.

## Primary metrics
- returning readers
- engaged reading
- newsletter conversion
- direct traffic
- branded search
- citations/backlinks
- data-tool repeat use

Commercial:
- sponsor renewal
- revenue per reader
- ad viewability without UX regression

Do not maximize impressions at the expense of return rate.
