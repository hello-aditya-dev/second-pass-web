# Brand System

## Name
Display the masthead as **SECOND / PASS**.
Use `Second Pass` where slash punctuation is impractical.

## Core idea
FIRST PASS = what happened.
SECOND PASS = what changed underneath, why it matters, what follows.

## Editorial labels
`/ NOW`, `/ SECOND PASS`, `/ DEEP`, `/ PROOF`, `/ DATA`, `/ BRIEF`, `/ SOURCES`, `/ CHANGE LOG`.

## Palette
- Paper `#F2EFE7`
- Ink `#11110F`
- Graphite `#5A5953`
- Rule `#C8C3B8`
- Signal blue `#2F5BFF`
- Signal blue dark `#1736A5`

Blue is for active states, links, evidence, important data, and the slash—not the whole page.

## Typography

### Bricolage Grotesque Variable
Masthead, headlines, navigation, section titles, display numbers.
Use its width/optical-size axes subtly for distinctive headline rhythm.

### Newsreader Variable
Long-form article body and long standfirsts.

### System monospace
Timestamps, evidence types, source labels, units, and changelog.

## Shape
Very low radius. Prefer rules, spacing, alignment, and typography to cards/shadows.

## Imagery
Prefer original charts, architecture diagrams, annotated screenshots, hardware, labs, people,
documents, and research figures where rights permit.

Avoid robot heads, glowing brains, generic servers, and decorative AI art.
