# SEO / Discover / News

Every article needs:
- stable slug
- title/dek
- real author identity
- datePublished
- truthful dateModified
- section
- canonical
- Article/NewsArticle JSON-LD
- Open Graph
- source area
- correction/update handling

Site:
- sitemap
- news sitemap
- RSS
- robots
- semantic HTML
- internal links
- fast rendering

Do not fake freshness or mass-generate thin SEO pages.

The news sitemap contains only recent news-eligible entries, not the whole archive.
